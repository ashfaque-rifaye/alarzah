Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge · Fertility Challenge 01: Male Reproductive Health

The demo video (under 3 minutes) will be published here by 20 November 2026. Until then, try the live guest-mode prototype.

What the video will show:

  1. The private, anonymous check-in (Arabic and English).
  2. The Alzara Clip recording left and right skin temperature live, and the phone computing heat load and a position-dependent left/right pattern.
  3. Red-flag routing: sudden pain goes to emergency now; a lump goes to urology urgently.
  4. The Alzara Pod keeping a home-collected sample between 20 and 37 °C, with a countdown and an anonymous lab code.
  5. Clinician review before any referral, and the "how your data will not be used" screen.
